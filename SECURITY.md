## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our clinical informatics and bioinformatic systems very seriously. If you discover any security-related defect, vulnerability, or exposure within this platform, please **DO NOT** open a public GitHub Issue. Instead, report it privately to ensure patient and data integrity.

Please report any security vulnerabilities by contacting the primary maintainer directly:

* **Contact Name:** Dr. Cesar Abrahan Manzo Carvajal
* **Role:** Lead Clinical Informatics Researcher & Developer
* **Email:** [Tu Correo Electrónico Aquí]

### What to Include in Your Report
To help us patch the issue quickly, please provide:
1. A detailed description of the vulnerability.
2. The specific endpoint or module affected (e.g., NutriKen backend, KENRYU microRNA integration).
3. Steps to reproduce the issue or a proof-of-concept (PoC) script.

### Our Response Commitment
* **Acknowledgement:** You will receive a formal response within 48 to 72 hours acknowledging your report.
* **Triage & Patch:** We will evaluate the technical impact and coordinate a secure deployment patch on our Hugging Face Spaces environment as soon as possible.
