# Summary
KENRYU is an open-source, independent bioinformatic platform designed to optimize clinical decision support in nutritional genomics. The software integrates complex biological data pipelines to analyze the interaction between dietary compounds, metabolic pathways, and genomic expressions—specifically focusing on microRNA regulation networks. Developed using Python, it offers a scalable architecture to cross-reference localized clinical inputs with global bioinformatic databases.

# Statement of Need
In modern clinical oncology and metabolic research, understanding the profound impact of nutritional factors on gene expression is critical. However, most existing genomic tools are fragmented, targeted purely at laboratory sequencing, or restricted behind proprietary institutional walls. 

KENRYU bridges this gap by providing an accessible, unified, and independent Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) pipeline. It allows general practitioners, dietitians, and clinical researchers to model dietary interventions against metabolic biomarkers, predicting microRNA silencing behaviors without relying on heavy institutional server stacks. This decentralization accelerates exploratory oncological and nutritional research, making high-level computational biology actionable for frontline clinical consultation.

# State of the Field
Current platforms in the field, such as traditional genomic browsers or localized metabolic mapping scripts, generally lack a dedicated user-centric clinical interface tailored for immediate nutritional triage. KENRYU differentiates itself by shifting the focus from raw data sequencing to real-time, hook-based metabolic network analysis, presenting interactive diagnostic support layers.
